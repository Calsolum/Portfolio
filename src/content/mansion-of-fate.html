Visual Novel · In development

An abandoned mansion appeared overnight. Nobody knows why. Erica does what Erica always does — she drags everyone inside to find out.

That's how you end up trapped.

Mansion of Fate is a mystery visual novel with a layered playthrough structure. Your first run is a prologue — the choices feel real, but something is holding back. Finish it, and the mansion opens up fully. This time the choices matter. This time your friends can die. And this time, the mansion isn't done with you yet.

Features:

  • Multiple endings shaped by who survives across playthroughs
  • A hidden true ending that only reveals itself over time
  • A cast of four with distinct personalities that affect puzzle outcomes
  • A story that remembers what you did last time

Mansion of Fate is still in development. Follow ns-kt on itch.io for its release.

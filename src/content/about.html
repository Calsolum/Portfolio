Tariq Singh is a writer and developer based in Brampton, Ontario.

His writing spans interactive fiction, fantasy novels, mystery novellas, poetry, stage adaptations, and screenwriting. His games are built in Visual Novel Maker and RPG Maker and are still in development — the Mansion of Fate prologue is playable free on itch.io. His technical projects — a self-hosted smart kitchen system, a digital party game, a board game companion app, a Raspberry Pi game assistant, and a team planner for Fate/Grand Order — are all built from scratch.

This site collects that work in one place — games, poetry, fiction, scripts, and projects, most of it readable in full.

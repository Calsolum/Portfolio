Tariq Singh is an editor and writer based in Brampton, Ontario, with an Honours Bachelor in Creative Writing and Publishing from Sheridan College.

He has edited for publication at Mobile Gaming News Network and Savant-Garde, proofreading and line-editing articles and book reviews and keeping a multi-writer publishing schedule on time. At Sheridan he led the 30-member team that took the graduate anthology from submissions to print.

His own writing spans interactive fiction, fantasy novels, mystery novellas, poetry, stage adaptations, and screenwriting. His games are built in Visual Novel Maker and RPG Maker and are still in development — the Mansion of Fate prologue and Choices are playable free on itch.io.

A Computer Programming diploma sits behind the technical side. He builds his projects with Claude as the coding partner, writing the brief, making the design calls and testing on real devices: a self-hosted smart kitchen system, a digital party game, a board game companion app, a Raspberry Pi game assistant, a team planner for Fate/Grand Order, a finder for weekday food deals, and add-ons for a car dashcam.

He is open to editorial and writing roles, and to other work where clear writing and technical fluency both count.

RPG · Short Form

Some enemies will fight you. Others will talk. What you do next is up to you — but you won't get to take it back.

Choices is a short RPG built around a single idea: consequences are permanent. No going back to try the other option. Three rooms, two paths, one question — can you live with what you decided?

Features:

  • Branching encounters that can be resolved through combat or conversation
  • Permanent choices — once made, they cannot be undone
  • A short, complete experience designed to be replayed with different decisions

Choices is still in development. Follow ns-kt on itch.io for its release.

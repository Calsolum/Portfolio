🍺 Gather Your Party (2–6 Players)
⚔ How DrunkQuest Works

Goal: First player to reach Level 6 wins.

Setup: Each player picks a Hero Class and a Realm — both grant unique abilities. Everyone starts with 5 Treasure cards.

Each Turn: The active player draws a Monster. All players take turns playing Treasure cards to modify it. Then the active player drinks the total drink value to defeat it and gains +1 Level + Treasure.

Boss Monsters: The whole party drinks together. Everyone gets Treasure — but no one gains a level!

Treasure Types: Action · Instant · Interrupt · Permanent

🔞 For adults only · Drink responsibly · Know your limits
